Energy Use in Australian Households

Australian households rely on a wide range of electrical appliances every day, from refrigerators and washing machines to televisions and air conditioners. Understanding how much energy these appliances consume can help households make informed decisions about usage habits and potential upgrades to more efficient models.

📈
Market Trend

Rising Running Costs

Electricity tariffs in Australia have trended upward over recent years. Investing in energy-conscious appliances and managing usage peaks directly lowers quarterly utility bills.

⭐
Standards

Energy Star Ratings

The Australian Energy Rating Label allows consumers to compare the relative efficiency and estimated annual kWh consumption across televisions, refrigerators, and washers.

🔌
Vampire Power

Standby Power Draw

Appliances left continuously in passive standby mode can draw 5 to 20 watts uninterrupted, contributing up to 10% of typical annual household power bills without delivering active use.
